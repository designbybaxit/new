Founded by Abdul Basit

Design by Baxit is founded by Abdul Basit, who has eight years of experience building websites and solutions for businesses. Over those years he's worked with founders, local service providers and growing teams — and noticed the same pattern almost every time.

The businesses that struggled online rarely lacked effort. They lacked focus. Their website said one thing, their social said another, and nothing told a customer why they should choose them. Effort was going out, but nothing was compounding.

So the studio was built around a different starting point: your brand's own preferences. How you want to be seen, who you actually want to serve, and what makes buying from you the obvious call. Everything after that — the identity, the site, the campaigns — is built to serve that single answer.

That's where less noise, more growth comes from. Fewer scattered tactics. More of the work that actually brings customers to your door.

Our approach
