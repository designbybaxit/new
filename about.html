Eight years of websites

Abdul Basit has 8+ years building websites for businesses. The work sits on Elementor and WordPress, Shopify, Squarespace, and Wix: landing pages, business sites, online stores, and redesigns.

Design by Baxit is the name on that work. Clients are international, mainly in the United States. The studio is based in Pakistan (PKT), with flexible hours so a meeting can overlap a US time zone.

A project can be one thing, such as a logo or a booking link. It can also run the whole way: logo, brand kit, website, and a simple automation that sends a new lead to email or a sheet, or sets up WhatsApp Business.

People usually arrive from a small ad and message on WhatsApp. That conversation is where the scope and the price are agreed, before any work starts.
