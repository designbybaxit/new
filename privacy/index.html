Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Design by Baxit is a small web design studio run by Abdul Basit. This page explains, in plain English, what information we collect through designbybaxit.com and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • What you type into our forms: your business name, email address, current website (if you give one), your industry and your answer about what you want from a new site.
  • Where you came from: ad campaign tags in the link you clicked (utm_source, utm_campaign, utm_content) and the page you sent the form from.
  • Usage and ad data: if enabled, the Meta Pixel and Google Analytics 4 use cookies and similar technology to record page views and that a form was sent, along with device, browser and approximate location data.

How we use it

  • To make and send the free mockup you asked for, and to reply about it.
  • To understand which pages and ads work, and to show our ads to people likely to be interested (including measuring conversions on Meta and Google).

We don't sell your personal information. We won't add you to a newsletter without asking.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the site: Cloudflare (hosting), Resend or FormSubmit (delivering form emails), Gmail (our inbox), Meta and Google (analytics and ads). Each handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use an ad blocker, opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out add-on, and control Meta ad personalisation in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings.

How long we keep it

We keep lead emails for as long as they are useful for the conversation, and no longer than 2 years after our last contact, unless you become a client.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop emailing you, at any time. Email designbybaxit@gmail.com and we'll handle it within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights under local law.

Children

This site is for businesses and isn't directed at children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page.
