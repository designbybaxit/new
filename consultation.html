FAQ

Before You Book

Is the consultation really free?

Yes — completely. No card, no hidden trial. We'd rather spend 30 minutes finding out whether we're a fit than sell you something that isn't.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Nothing formal. If you have a website, social accounts or any analytics access, having them handy makes the review sharper — but it's not required.

What if my business is brand new?

Even better. Getting positioning and identity right before you spend on marketing saves a lot of money later. Early-stage sessions focus on foundations.

Will you pressure me into a project?

No. You'll get the summary either way, and you're free to act on it yourself or with another team. We only propose work when we genuinely think we're the best option.

How soon can we start if it goes well?

Typically within one to two weeks, depending on current commitments. We take on a limited number of projects at a time so each gets proper attention.
