FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us.

Do you charge extra for evenings or weekends?

No. Our prices are the same 7 days a week. Club members also skip emergency fees.

How do I know what it will cost?

You'll see a typical range when you book online. On site, the technician confirms a flat-rate price, and you approve it before any work begins.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Larger jobs such as water heaters and HVAC systems can be paid monthly, subject to approval.

Are your technicians employees?

Yes. Every technician is a licensed, background-checked, drug-tested employee. Never a subcontractor.
