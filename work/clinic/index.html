FAQ

Good questions.

Can't see yours? Call us at (555) 010-2040.

Do you accept my insurance?

We work with most major PPO plans and file the claim for you. Send us your details and we'll check your benefits before your visit.

What if I'm nervous about the dentist?

You're in good company. Tell us when you book and we'll plan extra time, explain each step, and offer comfort and sedation options.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Interest-free monthly plans are available for treatments over $500, and we accept HSA and FSA cards.

How quickly can I be seen for an emergency?

We keep emergency slots open every weekday. Call before 11 AM and we'll usually see you the same day.
