Questions

How much does it cost to hire you?

Nothing up front. We work on a contingency fee, so we're only paid if we recover money for you.

How long do I have to file a claim?

Deadlines vary by state and case type, and some are short. Call us as soon as possible so evidence is preserved.

Should I talk to the other driver's insurance?

It's best not to give a recorded statement before speaking with a lawyer. We can handle all communication for you.

What is my case worth?

It depends on your injuries, lost income and long-term impact. We'll give you an honest assessment at your free consultation.
